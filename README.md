# The Excuse That Almost Works

The one line a gamer texts the boss to skip work on GTA 6 launch week.

> My wifi died mid-update and IT says the router's firmware bricked, they're coming to my flat sometime before lunch, probably.

**Character count: 125** (gate: 1–140 characters, single line; 20 words, the contest maximum).

## Why it lands

It starts as the most boring, believable IT disaster a manager has heard a hundred times — a dead router, a bricked firmware update — and then quietly ruins itself: IT does not do home visits to your flat, and the trailing "probably" is the tell of someone who just invented the whole thing on the spot. The manager almost believes it right up until that last word, which is exactly what the contest asks for. It also reads like a real hurried text — no greeting, no signature, no explanation of what "mid-update" even means — and it dodges every obvious excuse: no dead grandmother, no flat tyre, no sudden fever.
